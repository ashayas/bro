# Physical device checklist

Everything here needs real radios and couldn't be verified in the Simulator.

For a true Bluetooth test, turn **Same Wi-Fi** off in the app under Me on every phone.

## Two iPhones

- [ ] Both open, screens on: they see each other within about 15 seconds.
- [ ] Airplane mode with Bluetooth back on: still works.
- [ ] Strip, direct and crew bros all arrive. Direct shows "Bro'd".
- [ ] Both code only with no shared code: neither sees the other.
- [ ] Share a code: both appear in the crew.
- [ ] Scan the QR with the Camera: the join prompt opens.
- [ ] Lock one phone while linked, bro it: notification with sticker.
- [ ] Lock both for 10 minutes, wake one, bro: it arrives.
- [ ] Walk out of range, send a direct bro, walk back: it arrives.
- [ ] Bluetooth off and on in Settings: relinks without relaunch.
- [ ] Quit and reopen the app on one phone: relinks.

## Three or more

- [ ] A and C out of range with B between: A reaches C.
- [ ] Eight or more phones: everyone gets a Strip bro.

## Also

- [ ] iOS 15 or 16 device: every screen. Only iOS 18.6 and 26.3 were tested.
- [ ] iPhone 17 family: background discovery. Developers have reported iOS 26 scanning problems on these.
- [ ] Deny Bluetooth, then local network: the warning opens Settings.
- [ ] Silent switch on: no sound. Off: every mood says bro.
- [ ] An hour in the foreground and eight locked: battery is acceptable.
