# Before the App Store

- **Name:** search App Store and trademarks for "Bro Chat". It's generic, and may be taken.
- **Brand:** Brody is an original character. Keep other franchises' names, logos, characters and art out of the app, keywords and marketing.
- **Art:** the stickers and scenes were made with OpenAI image generation, using only text prompts and our own master image as reference (`Design/`). Purely generated art may not qualify for copyright protection in the US. If owning Brody matters, have an illustrator redraw him.
- **Guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality):** one-word apps get challenged. Lead the review notes with offline mesh, crews and stickers, and attach a video of two phones in airplane mode, since a reviewer with one device will see an empty radar.
- **Guideline 1.2 (user content):** there is no free text or media, and blocking is built in.
- **Age rating:** the party and hungover stickers suggest alcohol. Answer that question honestly.
- **Export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is false on the assumption that standard CryptoKit algorithms qualify as exempt. Confirm before submitting.
- **Privacy label:** nothing is collected.
